# 最佳报价 / Best Offer 1.6.1

- 交易设置使用“修改快捷键”标题，按钮显示“面板快捷键”和“诊断快捷键”；操作说明改为标题和按钮的鼠标悬停提示。点击后按 F1–F12，支持 Ctrl、Alt、Shift 组合；Esc 取消。结合按键事件与游戏逐帧输入，准确保留快速组合键中的修饰键。
- Mod 管理器增加面板与只读诊断快捷键。改键后点击“应用”，与交易设置同步并自动保存，重启后保留。
- 检查面板与诊断的重复绑定，并保留 Esc 的关闭／取消用途；无效配置保留原设置。
- 摆货未完成提示显示当前面板快捷键。默认仍为面板 F8、诊断 F9。
- 快捷键整行在上下分隔线之间垂直居中；悬停提示显示在按钮下方，提示文字垂直居中。

退出游戏后替换 `Mods/BestOffer.dll`，保留 `UserData` 中已有设置和规则。

## English

- Trade Settings groups Panel Hotkey and Diagnostics Hotkey under Edit Hotkeys. Instructions appear on hover over the heading or buttons. Key events and per-frame input preserve fast modifier combinations; press F1–F12 with optional Ctrl, Alt and Shift. Esc cancels capture.
- Mod Manager exposes panel and diagnostics hotkeys. Click Apply after editing. Both editors synchronize and persist across restarts.
- Duplicate bindings, invalid settings and reserved Esc bindings are rejected. Placement notices show the configured panel hotkey.
- Defaults remain F8 for the panel and F9 for read-only diagnostics.
- The hotkey row is centered between its upper and lower dividers. Hover instructions appear below the buttons, with vertically centered text.

Exit the game and replace `Mods/BestOffer.dll`. Keep your existing settings and rules.
