## Nexus 更新说明（可直接复制）

```text
1.7.3 更新

新增功能
• 一键摆货快捷键：默认 Ctrl+F8，自动计算最佳方案并摆货，不自动成交。
• 一键售卖快捷键：默认 Ctrl+F9，按一次直接计算并出售最佳方案，无需再次确认。
• 面板、诊断、摆货、售卖四项快捷键均可在游戏 Mods 齿轮或交易设置中修改，两处同步保存；每项都可清除禁用，重启保留。

修复与调整
• 修复正式版 3D 打印机输出武器需要勾选“机器”才能被识别的问题，现由“产物”选项正确控制。
• 同步适配其他机器的独立成品输出栏；电池、模块、芯片与原料仍归“机器”，空液体输出瓶继续作为设备保留。
• 四项快捷键统一归入“修改快捷键”，均提供清除按钮。

交易快捷键需在买家可交易且原版交易平板已打开时使用，继续遵守库存保护、分类规则、买家限制和预算。更新无需删除配置或存档。
如仍出现问题，请提供游戏版本、Mod 清单和截图，便于复现。

Best Offer 1.7.3

• Add Auto-fill (Ctrl+F8) and Quick Sell (Ctrl+F9) hotkeys. Quick Sell calculates and completes the best offer without another confirmation.
• Configure all four bindings through the native Mods gear or Trade Settings. Both editors stay in sync; any binding can be cleared/disabled across restarts.
• Fix printed weapons being classified as machine contents and recognize other dedicated finished-output inventories. Batteries, modules, chips and inputs remain under Machines; empty liquid-output bottles stay protected as equipment.
• Give every binding a Clear button under Edit Hotkeys.

Trading hotkeys require a tradable buyer and the native trading tablet to be open. Protection rules, buyer restrictions and budget limits still apply. Keep your existing settings and saves when updating. Please include your game version, mod list and screenshots if a problem persists.
```
